Privacy Policy

ClearStep Finance · Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short: ClearStep Finance keeps everything you enter on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The developer cannot see your information.

This policy explains how the ClearStep Finance mobile app (“the app”) handles information. The app is published by Dick Pere Ilaye (“I”, “me”).

1. Information you enter

The app lets you record and manage, as you choose:

  • Finances: income, expenses, credit card entries and limit, dates, categories and notes.
  • Student records: names, contact details (for example a phone number or Telegram handle), class or grade, level, lesson days and times, lesson rates and status.
  • Progress reports: lesson dates, topics, study materials and your notes.
  • Preferences: theme, calendar colors and lesson reminder settings.

All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. It is not sent to me or to any server operated by me, and I do not receive a copy.

2. Information I do not collect

The app does not require an account or login. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, does not set tracking cookies, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

3. Internet connections

The app works offline. When your device is online, the app loads decorative background photos from Unsplash (images.unsplash.com). To deliver an image, Unsplash's servers receive the technical information any web request includes, such as your IP address and device or browser type. No data you entered in the app is included in these requests. Unsplash handles that technical information under its own privacy policy. The app makes no other network connections.

4. Lesson reminders (notifications)

If you turn on Lesson Reminders, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are created on your device from the lesson days and times in your Student Record and are shown by your phone's own notification system. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

5. Backups you create

The Save button creates a backup file containing your app data and opens your phone's share sheet. You decide where the file goes (for example Files, a cloud drive, email or a messaging app). Once you share the file, it is handled by the service you chose, under that service's terms. Load reads a backup file that you select. I do not receive backup files.

Your phone's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may also include the app's data, depending on your device settings. That is governed by Apple or Google and your settings.

6. Information about other people

If you enter information about other people, such as your students or their parents or guardians, you are responsible for having the right to do so and for following the privacy laws that apply to you. Please keep such records to what you need.

7. Security

Your data stays within the app's private storage on your device, which is protected by your device's security. The app does not add its own password or encryption, so please protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep backup files somewhere safe.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can edit or delete any entry in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from your device. Because I hold no copy, I cannot recover data you have deleted or lost, which is why making your own backups is recommended.

9. Children

The app is a tool for tutors and adults managing their own finances and schedules. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, I will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
